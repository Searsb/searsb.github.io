# Welcome to Bryan Sears' Pages

### About Me

Professionally I work in cybersecurity. I am currently an IT Security Analyst at Ruoff Mortgage. I am currently [OSCP](https://www.credly.com/badges/e87bf65e-918b-4658-b7f6-f9a39538dadc/public_url) and [Security+](https://www.credly.com/badges/c8ad38bf-4ff6-4554-b7c4-e33c2ae6d3cd/public_url) certified. I am currently Studying for the OSEP/PEN-300 to advance my pentesting skills. I am on [Hack The Box](https://app.hackthebox.com/profile/467637) as well though I have stepped back a little to focus on the PEN-300. I am an advisor to Trine University's Hacking Team. As an Advisor I gave talks and advice and help to upcoming cybersecurity experts! I graduated from Trine University in May of 2022 with a Bachelors of Science Computer Science and Information Technology(CSIT). 

I grew up in Angola, Indiana. I played Football, Wrestling, and Track throughout High School. Now that I have graduated I have my official's license in Football and Wrestling. I also know how to run the timing camera for Track.

I volunteer at [Lake James Christian Camp](https://www.ljca.org/). I used to be lifeguard now I help with IT related issues and other general volunteer tasks during the summer.

I am also a Ham Radio technician my call sign is KD9QHL. I particpate in [Fort Wayne Radio Club's](https://fwrc.info/) Fox Hunts. A fox hunt is an event where you hunt for a radio transciever hidden somewhere in the county.
